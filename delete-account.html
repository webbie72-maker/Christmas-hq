Request account and data deletion

Christmas HQ is operated by Troy Webb. You can request deletion without reinstalling the app.

How to request

  1. Email Webbie72@live.com.au from the email address used for your Christmas HQ account, with the subject “Christmas HQ account deletion request”.
  2. The operator may need to verify that you own the account. Do not send your password or pattern.
  3. The operator will review associated information and family ownership, carry out deletion and let you know when it is complete. This is a manual request process; your account remains until processing finishes.

You can also sign in and use Settings → Account & data deletion → Request account deletion.

Information covered

The request covers your account and profile, family memberships and invitations, and associated cloud information such as your shared contributions, messages and uploaded music. If you organise a family, the operator will review how to preserve other members' information before removing your account.

Separate copies and retention

Personal lists, cached content and downloaded music on your device, exported backups, and copies saved by other people are separate from your cloud account. Clear local browser or app storage if you want to remove local copies; export anything you want to keep first. Deletion may affect shared conversations and Secret Santa draws.

Ask the operator about the expected processing time and any provider-backup or required-retention limitations. Any applicable retained information and the reason for retaining it should be explained when your request is processed.
