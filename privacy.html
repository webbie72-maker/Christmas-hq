Christmas HQ privacy policy

Updated 6 October 2026

Christmas HQ helps you organise gifts, food, plans and festive activities. This page explains the information used by its personal planning, family sharing and chat features.

Who to contact

Christmas HQ is operated by Troy Webb. For privacy questions, corrections or requests about your information, email Webbie72@live.com.au. Do not send your password or lock pattern.

Information you provide

  • Account email, display name and authentication information when you register or sign in.
  • Family membership and invitations, and names used to find or invite other users.
  • Gifts, prices, budgets, shopping lists, guests, events, saved locations, notes, recipes, menus, Santa letters and Secret Santa information you enter.
  • Food allergies and dietary requirements you choose to record. These can include sensitive health information.
  • Community posts, family conversations, private messages, reports and message-read information.
  • Music files and preferences when you use music features, including files you choose to upload or back up.

Personal plans and device storage

Personal planning data, preferences, cached content and the device lock are stored in your browser or on your device. Clearing browser storage or removing the app can remove locally stored information. Exported backups contain the information described on the backup screen, which can include food requirements, Secret Santa assignments and audio. Keep backups private.

Family sharing and chat

Account and shared features send information to cloud services. Shared family lists and family conversations are intended for connected family members. Community posts are visible to other community users. Direct messages are intended for the sender and recipient; they are not described as end-to-end encrypted. Hosting and database administrators may have access to stored information for operating, supporting or investigating the service.

Display names and account identifiers may be used for member search, invitations and conversations. Only enter information about other people that you are entitled to share. Avoid posting private contact details or sensitive information in community chat.

How information is used

Information supports sign-in, saving and restoring plans, family synchronisation, invitations, chat, unread indicators, music and handling reports or support requests. Service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times and device or browser information to deliver and protect their services.

Service providers and external links

Christmas HQ uses Vercel hosting and Supabase account and data services. Some software loads from jsDelivr and unpkg. Providers may process information outside Australia; their own privacy practices also apply. External searches, maps, shops, streaming guides, Facebook and sharing apps receive information when you use those services. Information included in an external search or shared invitation is sent to the service you select.

Camera and permissions

Barcode scanning requests camera access when used. You can decline or revoke camera access through your browser or device settings. Enter a suburb or address manually for map searches if you prefer. An operating-system share sheet sends the selected information to your chosen destination.

Keeping and removing information

You can submit an account-deletion request in Settings or use our public account-deletion request page. Requests are manually processed by the operator; submission is not immediate deletion.

You can remove supported list items and use the personal-plan reset or browser-storage controls. Resetting a personal plan does not delete your cloud account, shared family information or chat history. Leaving a family is also different from deleting an account.

To request access, correction, or deletion of account information, email Webbie72@live.com.au from your account email and describe your request. Identity may need to be verified before action is taken. Copies held by message recipients, other users or in exported backups may remain outside the operator's control. Ask about any applicable retention or backup limitations when making a request.

Children and family information

Adults should supervise children's use and enter only the information needed for planning. Avoid including a child's full name, home address, school or other identifying details in community posts or Santa letters. A local pattern lock is a casual privacy feature, not a replacement for account security.

Changes

This page may be updated as features and information handling change. The revision date above identifies the current version. Contact the operator if you have a concern about information handling.
