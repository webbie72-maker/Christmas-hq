CHRISTMAS HQ — ELF ON THE SHELF TAB

UPLOAD
1. Extract this ZIP first.
2. Open webbie72-maker/Christmas-hq on GitHub.
3. Add file > Upload files. Upload these FOUR files beside index.html:
   index.html
   service-worker.js
   elf-on-the-shelf.js
   elf-setup-pictures.webp
4. Replace the existing index.html and service-worker.js, then Commit changes.
5. Once Vercel deploys, reopen the app online and go to Magic > Elf on the Shelf.

INCLUDED
24 relevant setup ideas with matched illustrative pictures, supplies and steps.
Filters: quick (5 minutes), no mess, no shopping, funny, toddler-friendly.
24 morning slots for 1–24 December, with a year selector and completion ticks.
Save one idea to a date or fill only empty dates. Existing plans are preserved.
Separate traditional story guide and editable family rules.
Elf name, optional child/family name, touching preference and story style.
Editable arrival/goodbye notes with save and print.

SAVING
Elf plans, rules and notes are saved on this device, separately for the signed-in account or the signed-out device view. They do not sync to Family HQ or appear in the existing plan export. Changing phones requires setting them up there.
